Core capabilities

What you get.

Six pillars that make this system different from black-box models.

Physics-Informed AI

Physical laws are constraints in the loss function, not afterthoughts.

  • Water balance equations
  • Nutrient cycling dynamics
  • Energy conservation
  • Crop coefficient models (FAO-56)

Explainable Reasoning

Every advisory shows the factors that drove it.

  • Feature attributions per prediction
  • Physics residuals for auditing
  • Human-readable explanations
  • Full inference audit trail

Climate-Smart

Optimized for the realities Kenyan farmers face every season.

  • Drought alerts
  • Flood warnings
  • Variable-rainfall planning
  • Seasonal forecast integration

Sparse-Data Ready

Physics fills the gaps where historical data can't.

  • Works in low-data regions
  • Nationwide coverage
  • Accurate at county level
  • Gap-filling via physical constraints

Reaches Every Farmer

Multi-channel delivery that works without a smartphone.

  • iShamba SMS (≤320 chars)
  • Selector Platform delivery
  • County extension officers
  • USSD and voice-ready payloads

Measurable Impact

Every recommendation is tracked from issue to outcome.

  • Yield improvement tracking
  • Fertilizer efficiency metrics
  • Farmer feedback loop
  • Delivery + outcome analytics
Platform architecture

Five stages from data to action.

Every advisory passes through the same auditable pipeline.

1

Ingest

KAOP weather, AgData Hub soil & prices, KALRO trial database, farmer registry.

2

Normalize

Standardized schemas, quality control, geospatial alignment, deduplication.

3

Constrain

Physics-informed neural network with water, nutrient, and energy constraints.

4

Explain

Feature attributions, physics residuals, and human-readable reasoning.

5

Deliver

SMS, Selector Platform, extension officers — with feedback loop.

Built for every role

Three audiences, one platform.

Farmers, extension officers, and researchers each get a workspace shaped around their work.

For Farmers

Personalized advisories for your farm, crop, and county — delivered by SMS and web.

  • Plant, fertilize, and harvest guidance
  • Weather and drought warnings
  • Pest and disease alerts
  • Market price updates

For Extension Officers

County-scoped field data, farmer registration, and advisory delivery.

  • Register and manage farmers
  • Record soil tests and farm details
  • GPS-map farms in the county
  • Deliver advisories in person

For Researchers

Train PINN models, analyze trials, and audit physics residuals.

  • Train and version PINN models
  • Analyze fertilizer trial results
  • Inspect inference residuals
  • Export data for publication
Why it's different

Not another black-box model.

How KALRO Advisory compares to conventional machine learning systems.

Criterion Conventional ML KALRO Advisory (PINN)
Physical consistency Not guaranteed — predictions can violate physical laws Enforced via constraints in the loss function
Explainability Black box — reasoning opaque Feature attributions + physics residuals per prediction
Sparse-data performance Degrades sharply where data is thin Physics fills the gaps — works in low-data regions
Auditability Hard to trace how a decision was made Full inference audit trail with residuals
Delivery to farmers Requires smartphone or internet SMS, Selector, USSD — no smartphone required
Data integration Isolated datasets, manual joins KAOP, AgData Hub, trials, registry — unified
