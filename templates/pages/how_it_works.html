End-to-end flow

How an advisory is built.

Every stage is auditable and every step is logged for KALRO researchers.

1

Ingest

From KALRO systems

Data flows in from the systems KALRO already runs — no new sensors required. Weather, soil, market, and trial data arrive on schedule and are normalized into a single schema for downstream processing.

  • KAOP weather & forecasts
  • AgData Hub soil tests
  • KALRO fertilizer trial database (150K+)
  • Market prices & farmer registry
2

Constrain

Physics-informed model

Physical laws are embedded directly into the model's loss function, so predictions stay physically plausible even with sparse historical data. This is what separates a PINN from a conventional ML model.

  • Water balance (dS/dt = P − ET − R − D)
  • Nutrient cycling constraints
  • Energy conservation
  • Crop growth stage coefficients
3

Infer

Farm-level recommendation

The PINN produces a farm-level recommendation plus a full explanation — not just an output number. Every prediction carries its reasoning chain so farmers and extension officers can see the basis.

  • Recommended fertilizer rates (N, P, K)
  • Feature attributions per advisory
  • Physics residuals (consistency check)
  • Human-readable reasoning chain
4

Deliver

Multi-channel outreach

Advisories reach farmers through channels they already trust. The feedback loop captures outcomes and continuously improves the model.

  • iShamba SMS (no smartphone required)
  • Selector Platform (county networks)
  • Extension officers on the ground
  • Farmer feedback captured & scored
Embedded physics

The laws in the loss function.

Three physical constraints anchor every prediction to reality.

Water Balance
Soil · Atmosphere
dS/dt = P − ET − R − D

Soil moisture change equals precipitation minus evapotranspiration, runoff, and deep drainage. Anchors irrigation and drought advisories.

Nitrogen Cycling
Soil · Plant
dN/dt = F + M − U − L

Soil nitrogen changes based on fertilizer input, mineralization, crop uptake, and leaching. Anchors fertilizer-recommendation advisories.

Energy Conservation
Atmosphere
Rn = LE + H + G

Net radiation equals latent, sensible, and ground heat flux. Anchors temperature and heat-stress advisories.
