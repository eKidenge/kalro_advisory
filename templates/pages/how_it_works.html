The Pipeline

From climate data to farm decisions.

Four stages. One outcome: an advisory a farmer can act on today, with the reasoning behind it exposed.

End-to-end flow

How an advisory is built.

Each stage is auditable, and every step is logged for KALRO researchers.

1

Ingest

Data flows in from the systems KALRO already runs — no new sensors required.

  • KAOP weather & forecasts
  • AgData Hub soil tests
  • KALRO fertilizer trial database
  • Market prices & farmer registry
2

Constrain

Physical laws are embedded directly into the model's loss function, so predictions stay physically plausible even with sparse data.

  • Water balance (dS/dt = P − ET − R − D)
  • Nutrient cycling constraints
  • Energy conservation
  • Crop growth stage coefficients
3

Infer

The PINN produces a farm-level recommendation plus a full explanation — not just an output number.

  • Recommended fertilizer rates (N, P, K)
  • Feature attributions per advisory
  • Physics residuals (consistency check)
  • Human-readable reasoning chain
4

Deliver

Advisories reach farmers through channels they already trust — and the feedback loop keeps improving the model.

  • iShamba SMS (no smartphone required)
  • Selector Platform (county networks)
  • Extension officers on the ground
  • Farmer feedback captured & scored
