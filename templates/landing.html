What makes it different

Not another black-box model.

Traditional machine learning fails where data is sparse and answers are opaque. We embed physical laws directly into the AI.

Physics-Informed AI

Water balance, nutrient cycling, and energy conservation are constraints in the loss function — not afterthoughts.

Explainable Reasoning

Every advisory comes with the factors that drove it. Farmers and extension officers can see why, not just what.

Climate-Smart

Optimized for drought, flood, and variable rainfall — the realities Kenyan farmers face every season.

Sparse-Data Ready

Works in regions with limited historical records. Physics fills the gaps where data can't.

Reaches Every Farmer

Delivered via iShamba SMS and the Selector Platform. No smartphone required.

Measurable Impact

Targeting 15–20% yield increase through improved fertilizer efficiency and timely advisories.

How it works

From climate data to farm decisions.

1

Ingest

Weather from KAOP, soil and market data from AgData Hub, fertilizer trials from KALRO's database.

2

Infer

The PINN engine blends machine learning with physical constraints to produce farm-level recommendations with full explainability.

3

Deliver

Advisories go out via SMS, Selector Platform, and county extension officers — with a feedback loop that keeps improving the model.

Built on KALRO's existing infrastructure.
